# Chapter 1: Accelerate

## Core Idea
Software delivery performance is a competitive differentiator for every organization; the key to improving it is investing in measurable capabilities — not chasing maturity models.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Capabilities Model vs. Maturity Model**: Maturity models declare an organization "done" and prescribe lock-step linear progression. Capability models focus on continuous improvement, are multidimensional, and allow each team to focus on what drives the most value in their context.
  - When to use: Use the capability lens whenever assessing DevOps progress; reject "level 3" or "level 4" framing.
  - How: Identify 24 measurable capabilities, track improvement in each over time, and tie capability growth to outcome metrics.

## Key Concepts
- **Capability model**: A framework that measures specific, improvable behaviors linked to outcomes — not a static maturity level.
- **DevOps**: Practices enabling secure, resilient, rapidly evolving distributed systems; originated from web-scale organizations but applicable across all industries and tech stacks.
- **Evidence-based transformation**: Using empirical research (not vendor preferences or consultant intuition) to decide which capabilities to invest in.

## Mental Models
- Think of the 24 capabilities as levers — each one you improve turns up performance on one or more outcome dials.
- "The highest performers are always striving to be better and never consider themselves 'mature' or 'done.'" — continuous improvement has no finish line.

## Anti-patterns
- **Maturity model thinking**: Declaring success at "Level X" halts improvement and fails to adapt to changing business and technology contexts.
- **Vanity metrics**: Measuring tool install base or certification counts rather than capability impact on outcomes.
- **Overestimating DevOps maturity**: Executives consistently rate their organizations higher than practitioners do; close the gap by measuring actual outcomes.

## Key Takeaways
1. Software is a key differentiator regardless of industry — delivery performance matters for banks, retailers, and government alike.
2. Focus on capabilities and outcomes, not on prescriptive maturity levels.
3. The 24 capabilities identified through four years of rigorous research are the right levers to pull.
4. High performers never stop improving; the gap between high and low performers continues to widen year over year.
